### One platform for the entire event journey

From the first announcement to the final evaluation, every step lives in a structured workflow instead of scattered spreadsheets and phone calls.

### What the platform covers

- **Event creation & configuration** — setup phases, tracks, capacities, and access rules
- **Participant registration** — forms, confirmations, waitlists, and attendee records
- **Dynamic scheduling** — sessions, rooms, speakers, and conflict-aware timetables
- **Evaluations & feedback** — post-session surveys and structured rating workflows
- **Secure online payments** — registration fees and paid sessions with audit trails
- **Live broadcasting** — integrated streaming for remote and hybrid audiences
- **Admin dashboards** — operational overview for organizers and support teams
- **Automated notifications** — email and SMS updates across the event timeline
- **External service connections** — payment, streaming, and communication providers

### Operational impact

Events succeed when nothing falls through the cracks. This platform gives organizers confidence that registrations, schedules, payments, and broadcasts stay synchronized — even when last-minute changes happen.
