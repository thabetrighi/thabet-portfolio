### Every trainee journey, tracked end to end

From first registration to final exam — one system instead of paper folders.

### Operational coverage

- **Trainee registration** — intake, documents, and training program assignment
- **Training & lesson scheduling** — instructors, vehicles, slots, and conflict prevention
- **Attendance tracking** — session presence linked to progress requirements
- **Examination management** — scheduling, results, and retry rules
- **Performance tracking** — skill milestones and instructor notes
- **Reports** — trainee progress, instructor workload, and school metrics
- **Invoicing** — fee generation aligned with training stages
- **Online payments** — secure collection for fees and packages
- **Administrative workflows** — approvals, exceptions, and daily operations

### School operations, simplified

Administrators see who needs a lesson, who is exam-ready, and who owes fees — without chasing spreadsheets.
