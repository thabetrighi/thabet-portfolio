### Structure for professional consultation services

Clients need clarity on availability. Consultants need protected time. Admins need oversight.

### Core features

- **Consultant profiles & management** — specialties, availability rules, and service definitions
- **Calendar synchronization** — align external calendars with booking availability
- **Appointment scheduling** — self-service booking with confirmations and reminders
- **Secure video consultations** — protected sessions for remote meetings
- **Client management** — history, notes, and communication records
- **Automated client communications** — status updates across the consultation journey
- **Workflow management** — request, confirm, conduct, and close consultations systematically
- **Backend services & admin tools** — operational control and reporting for support teams

### Client experience wins

When booking is simple and sessions are secure, trust grows. The platform replaces back-and-forth messages with a professional, predictable flow.
