### Grants demand rigor, not improvisation

Every approval, budget line, and signature must be auditable.

### Platform capabilities

- **Grant application management** — intake, review stages, and applicant communication
- **Budget management** — allocations, revisions, and spending visibility
- **Contract management** — templates, versions, and obligation tracking
- **Workflow management** — configurable stages across departments
- **Institutional system integration** — sync financial data with enterprise ERP
- **Digital signatures** — legally traceable signing within approval flows
- **Approval processes** — multi-level authorization with full audit trails
- **Financial workflows** — disbursement rules and compliance checkpoints
- **Administrative dashboards** — pipeline visibility for program managers
- **Third-party connections** — external verification and reporting services

### Institutional trust

When money and compliance are involved, systems must be predictable. This platform gives administrators confidence that nothing bypasses policy.
